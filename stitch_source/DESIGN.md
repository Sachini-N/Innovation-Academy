## Brand & Style
The design system reflects the stature of an established national innovation catalyst. It blends institutional authority with forward-looking technological vision, speaking directly to C-suite executives, global technology investors, policymakers, and pioneering entrepreneurs.

The aesthetic follows **Corporate / Modern** principles elevated by architectural precision and subtle executive polish:
- **Tone:** Authoritative, distinguished, visionary, and meticulously engineered.
- **Visual Stance:** Structured grid frameworks, generous negative space, deliberate borders, and crisp contrasts. The experience avoids chaotic startup tropes or playful gimmicks, emphasizing stability, scale, and high-trust credibility.
- **Metaphor:** Architectural transparency—inspired by modern industrial research campuses, precision glass, structural slate, and illuminated sapphire accents representing technological advancement.

## Colors
The color architecture reinforces national prestige, technological prowess, and uncompromised clarity:

- **Primary Deep Navy (`#0B192C`):** Conveys governance, historical depth, and institutional foundation. Used for primary navigation, deep grounding panels, key interactive CTA surfaces, and monumental headlines.
- **Secondary Refined Cyan-Sapphire (`#0284C7`):** Represents dynamic forward momentum and technological clarity. Applied to high-priority interactive states, micro-indicators, and structural focus treatments.
- **Tertiary Royal Blue (`#2563EB`):** Serves as a radiant accent for visionary achievements, active indicators, and high-impact data highlights.
- **Neutral Charcoal (`#0F172A`):** The foundational text color, delivering optimal contrast without the jarring harshness of pure black.
- **Surface Foundations:**
  - `surface-canvas`: `#FFFFFF` for primary canvas readouts.
  - `surface-subtle`: `#F8FAFC` for alternate section blocks and alternating table bands.
  - `surface-container`: `#F1F5F9` for structured card baselines, secondary badges, and architectural division.
  - `border-subtle`: `#E2E8F0` for hairline 1px architectural grid divisions.
  - `border-strong`: `#CBD5E1` for actionable containment and active input perimeters.

## Typography
The system balances structural authority with effortless legibility by pairing **Plus Jakarta Sans** for display, structural headings, and metrics with **Inter** for sustained technical reading, governance papers, and long-form narrative:

- **Display & Headings (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Crafted with precise optical negative tracking (`-0.03em` to `-0.015em`) and rigorous line geometry. Gives keynote statements, initiative titles, and impact numbers an authoritative, corporate voice.
- **Body & Data (Inter):** Highly legible, neutral workhorse font that keeps research papers, policy briefs, case studies, and partner metadata completely frictionless across both dense screen tables and narrative essays.
- **Badges & Labels (Plus Jakarta Sans Upper/Semi-bold):** Micro-labels utilize increased letter-spacing (`0.06em`) to preserve crisp character separation on dark badges and small metadata tags.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout adheres to a rigorous, disciplined architectural grid system reflecting stability and structured innovation:

- **Grid Structure:** 
  - **Desktop (>= 1200px):** 12-column grid, max content width 1280px, centered with `margin` of `3rem` and `gutter` of `1.5rem`.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column grid with `margin-tablet` of `2rem` and `gutter` of `1.5rem`.
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column fluid grid with `margin-mobile` of `1.25rem` and `gutter-mobile` of `1rem`.
- **Rhythm Philosophy:** Sections employ generous vertical breathing space (`space-2xl` to `space-3xl`) between major thematic blocks (e.g., Enterprise Initiatives, Board of Directors, Venture Portfolio), instilling executive calm. Component internals rely strictly on an 8-point base increment: `space-xs` (4px), `space-sm` (8px), `space-md` (16px), `space-lg` (24px), and `space-xl` (40px).

## Elevation & Depth
Depth in the design system conveys executive weight through **low-contrast outlines, tonal layering, and ultra-diffused ambient illumination**:

- **Ground & Tonal Surfaces:** Elevation is established first through surface variation rather than heavy drop shadows. Level 0 rests on `#FFFFFF` or `#F8FAFC`. Level 1 elements (cards, containers) sit atop surfaces accompanied by a crisp 1px border (`#E2E8F0`).
- **Ambient Shadow Crafting:** When floating elements occur (dropdowns, executive modals, active cards), shadows remain exceptionally diffused and color-tinted with primary deep navy:
  - *Resting Card Elevation:* `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.04)` with a perimeter 1px border in `#E2E8F0`.
  - *Hover / Focused Elevation:* `0 12px 24px -6px rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.08), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.03)` with a border shift to `#CBD5E1`.
  - *Modal & Executive Popover:* `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.16)`.
- **Structural Insets:** Highlight indicators and interactive status lines utilize 2px top or lateral accents in `#0284C7`, imparting an authoritative engineering demeanor.

## Shapes
The shape philosophy is built on precision and restraint (**Soft, Level 1**):

- **Standard Elements (Buttons, Inputs, Badges, Chips):** Default radius of `0.25rem` (4px). This creates crisp, tailored edges that avoid casual toy-like circularity and maintain an institutional silhouette.
- **Card Containers & Structural Blocks:** `rounded-lg` of `0.5rem` (8px) paired with 1px slate perimeter outlines.
- **Dialogs & Executive Overlays:** `rounded-xl` of `0.75rem` (12px), framing complex governance and application structures.
- **Badges & Credibility Seals:** Sharp micro-corner containment (`0.25rem`) or strict architectural rectangles with subtle 1px inner borders. Pill shapes are intentionally avoided for corporate governance artifacts.
